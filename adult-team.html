Climb With
A Team.

The adult team.

A standing weekly session for adults who want more out of the gym than a membership card. Same group, same night, a coach who learns your climbing, and a room full of people who notice when you send.

It works like a league — a season, a roster, a night that's yours — but nobody is keeping score. Nobody gets cut. You're not climbing against the person next to you, you're climbing next to them, and that turns out to be the fastest way to get better.

Climbing alone with headphones in will only take you so far. Most people plateau because they run the same problems the same way every visit and have nobody to tell them otherwise. A team fixes both halves of that: the coaching and the company.

Get on the list See the details

Every ability level.

There is no tryout, no minimum grade, and no experience requirement. If you can get up a ladder you can be on this team.

New to climbing

You've been a handful of times, you like it, and you're not sure what to do next. The team gives you structure, the fundamentals of movement, and a standing reason to show up on a night you'd otherwise talk yourself out of.

Climbing a while, stuck

You've been climbing the same grade for a year and you know it. You'll get honest eyes on your movement, drills aimed at the thing actually holding you back, and people who will keep you on a project past the point you'd normally walk away.

Climbing solo

You come in, you climb by yourself, you leave. Nothing wrong with that — but if you've been meaning to know people here and haven't found the door in, this is the door in.

Back after time away

Injury, a busy stretch, a few years off. Coming back alone is hard and a little demoralizing. Coming back with a group that expects you on Tuesday is a different experience entirely.

Note for Chris & Nicole

For reference only, not a recommendation: Catalyst charges £144 per 4 weeks for their adult teams, on top of gym entry. That's a UK market with different economics. Mosaic's whole positioning is best-value-in-the-metro, and adult memberships are $75/mo — a team priced anywhere near the membership itself will read wrong against that. Worth pricing this as an add-on that feels obviously worth it rather than as a second membership.

The other real constraint is coach hours. Mosaic's private instruction is $60/hr for one person and $45/hr each for 2–4. A weekly team session is a recurring block of a coach's time — the price needs to cover that at whatever team size we set the minimum to.

FAQS
Do I have to be good?

No. There's no tryout and no minimum grade. The team is built for a range of abilities climbing in the same room at the same time — that's normal and it's part of what makes it work.

Is this a competition team?

No. Nobody is scored, ranked, or cut. It's a team in the sense that you belong to a group that trains together — not in the sense that you're competing against anyone.

What if I miss a week?

DECIDE Catalyst has a freeze policy where you aren't charged for sessions you can't attend. That's generous and hard to administer on a season model. Simpler answer: life happens, come when you can, the season price is the season price.

Do I need my own gear?

No. Rentals are included with day passes and memberships at Mosaic.

Bouldering, ropes, or both?

DECIDE Bouldering-only is simpler to coach and needs no belay competency. Including ropes means every team member needs to be belay-checked, which is a real gate on the "all abilities, just show up" pitch — though Mosaic's belay and lead classes are complimentary, so it's a solvable one.
